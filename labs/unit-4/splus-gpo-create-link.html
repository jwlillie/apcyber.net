Lab — Create, Link, and Harden a Group Policy Object

SY0-701 · 4.5 Group Policy Endpoint hardening

Larkspur Analytics is onboarding a wave of seasonal contractors into its Engineering and Logistics teams, and the front Reception area runs shared kiosk PCs. Security has approved a hardened endpoint configuration and exported it to the security template endpoint_hardening.inf (stored in C:\SecBaselines on the domain controller LARK-DC1).

You are the security administrator. From the Group Policy Management console on the larkspur.local domain, complete the following:

  1. Create a GPO named Endpoint Hardening Baseline in the larkspur.local domain.
  2. Link that GPO to three locations: Engineering › Seasonal-Engineering, Logistics › Seasonal-Logistics, and Reception.
  3. Import the endpoint_hardening.inf template into the GPO's Security Settings.
