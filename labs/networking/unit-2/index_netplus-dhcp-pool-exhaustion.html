Out of Addresses: Diagnosing a DHCP Pool Exhaustion

N10-007 · 1.4N10-007 · 1.8N10-007 · 5.5 AP Networking · 2.1AP Networking · 2.3
Riverbend Commons is a small community makerspace and lending library. You volunteer as its network admin. Yesterday a teammate, Devon, was tidying up DHCP reservations on the Commons-WiFi scope and trimmed the address range while doing it. Today, visitors in the second-floor study area report a strange pattern: some laptops get online fine, others can't reach anything. The DHCP server is svc-dhcp01 on host rbc-core; the network is 10.20.40.0/24, gateway 10.20.40.1. Find out why only some devices fail, fix the cause, and confirm the fix.
