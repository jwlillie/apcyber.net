N10-009 · 1.5 N10-007 · 2.1 AP Networking · 2.3

You're the on-site cabling tech at Cedarwood Health Clinic. In second-floor closet TR-2, horizontal runs from three newly occupied rooms land on a 24-port copper patch panel, mounted above access switch SW-CEDAR-2. Bring each room online by running a patch lead from the right panel jack to the right switch port, then verify the links. A lead in the wrong port faults the same way it would in a real rack.
