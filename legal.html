Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 21, 2026  •  Last Updated: March 21, 2026  •  Site: apcyber.net

This site collects zero personal data

apcyber.net is a free, open educational resource. There are no accounts, no logins, no cookies, no tracking, and no analytics. Nothing you do on this site is recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Section 1

What We Collect

Nothing. This site does not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal information from visitors. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or any contact information
  • IP addresses or device identifiers
  • Browser fingerprints or geolocation data
  • Cookies or persistent identifiers of any kind
  • Usage data, page views, or session recordings
  • Quiz answers, scores, or any activity data
  • Student or teacher information of any kind
Section 2

How the Site Works

apcyber.net is a collection of static HTML files hosted on Netlify. All interactive features — quizzes, flashcards, scenario tools — run entirely within your browser. No data is sent to any server operated by this site.

The AI-powered features (Flashcard Generator, AI Tutor, Spot the Attack, Synthesis Quiz, Defense Builder, and Attack Chain Mapper) send the text you type into those tools to Anthropic’s Claude API to generate responses. This is the only outbound data transmission from this site. We have no access to these requests. Do not enter any personally identifiable information into AI prompt fields. Anthropic’s Privacy Policy governs their handling of API requests.

Google Fonts are loaded from Google’s CDN for typography. No analytics platform, advertising network, tracking pixel, or session recording tool is installed on this site.

Section 3

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of any kind. Your browser will not receive any cookie from apcyber.net.

Section 4

Advertising

There are no advertisements on this site. No ad networks, sponsored content, affiliate links, or paid placements exist anywhere on apcyber.net.

Section 5

Children’s Privacy (COPPA), FERPA & CIPA

COPPA: Because we collect no personal information from anyone — including children under 13 — COPPA’s parental consent requirements are not triggered.

FERPA: Because this site collects no student data and maintains no student records, FERPA does not apply to the operation of this site. No education records are created, stored, or maintained here.

CIPA: This site supports school CIPA compliance. It contains no social features, no user-generated public content, no advertising, no chat, and no links to content harmful to minors.

Section 6

Changes & Contact

If this site ever begins collecting any data, this policy will be updated before that change takes effect. Because there are no user accounts, we have no way to notify users directly — check this page for the current policy.

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed through the site at apcyber.net.
