Scenario

Northwind Cartage just made you a junior systems administrator. The company's first Windows domain controller, HQ-DC01, was promoted yesterday and the domain northwind.lan is now live. Every account currently lands in the default Users container, which makes delegating help-desk rights and linking per-department policy impossible. Before any accounts are migrated, your manager wants the directory reorganized to mirror the org chart — and wants accidental-deletion protection switched on for every OU so a stray right-click can't wipe out a whole department.
