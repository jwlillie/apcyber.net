Harden a Wireless Network

You're a contract security consultant brought in by Marsh & Halloran, a 24-person design studio. Their wireless is running on factory defaults and anyone in the building's shared lobby can associate with it. The office manager, Priya Anand, has given you console access to the new AeroLink AL-340 wireless controller and wants three things locked down today: the default administrator account, who is allowed to connect, and a standing rule to keep recreational devices off the corporate radio.
