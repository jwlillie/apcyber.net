SY0-701 4.6 — Identity & Access Management

Scenario

You administer the nwlogix.local domain for Northwind Logistics from the domain controller NWL-DC01. Resource permissions are currently assigned to people one account at a time, which is slow and error-prone. To simplify your access control lists, you will gather every shift-lead into a single group, then grant that group permission once.

Your tasks in the Directory Console:

  1. In the Users container, create a group named LogisticsLeads with Global scope and Security type.

    • Priya Nair — Dispatch \ DispatchLeads
