Scenario

You are the IT lead at Halvorsen Tax Group. A new Windows workstation, WS-AUDIT-14, was just issued to staff accountant Priya Nandakumar. Her casework lives on the firm's Records (R:) drive, and a second internal disk, Vault (V:), was added specifically to hold local recovery copies. Configure File History so a recent version of her work is always recoverable after accidental edits or a disk failure.
