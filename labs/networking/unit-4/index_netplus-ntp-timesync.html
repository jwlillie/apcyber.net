The job

Northwind Audio books three live rooms by the hour. The booking log lives on the Linux host nw-loghost; the front desk takes walk-ins on the Windows workstation NW-FRONTDESK. Their clocks have drifted apart, so the same session shows two start times and Studio B keeps getting double-booked.

Fix the drift the right way: make nw-loghost a working time source on the LAN, then point the front-desk workstation at it and confirm both ends agree. You're signed in as root on the Linux box and as the desk operator on Windows.
