Scenario

You are the security administrator at Meridian Field Services, a small surveying firm. You have stood up a remote-access IPSec VPN on the company's edge security appliance so field crews can reach internal resources from the road.

Tonight you are working from home over your own Wi-Fi, and you need to configure your company iPad to use the new VPN. The tablet is already on your home network.

Add the configuration under Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › VPN, then connect and confirm.
