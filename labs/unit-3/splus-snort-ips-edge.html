Edge IPS — Turn Detection into Prevention

Harborlight Outfitters · perimeter firewall hardening · Security+ SY0-701

Your role: You are the lone security administrator at Harborlight Outfitters, a small online retailer. The open-source Snort package is already installed on the company's perimeter pfSense firewall, but right now it only watches traffic — it never stops anything. Management wants the internet-facing link actively protected before the holiday sales rush. Your job is to download the detection rule sets, schedule automatic updates, and promote Snort from a passive listener into an inline intrusion-prevention system on the WAN interface.
