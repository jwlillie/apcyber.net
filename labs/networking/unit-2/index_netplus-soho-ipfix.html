The Loaner That Won't Reach the Internet

Cedar Hollow SOHO · Network connectivity troubleshooting lab
N10-009 · 5.1 N10-009 · 5.5 N10-009 · 5.6 AP NET · 1.1.A AP NET · 2.1.A AP NET · 2.1.B

You help run the tiny office at the Cedar Hollow Community Garden. A volunteer carried over a spare laptop, garden-loaner, from the downtown branch so people can sign up for plots online. On the garden's home-office network it gets no internet at all. The office desktop, office-desk, works fine on the same router, hollow-gw.

Work the problem with the methodology: gather information, compare the broken host to the working one, form a theory, fix it, then verify. Pick a host below, read its settings, and use the command line. Tasks tick off as you go.
