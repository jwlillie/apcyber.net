Incident brief

Larkspur Forums, a hobby site many staff reused passwords on, was breached. Its leaked credential dump reached Tarnhelm Logistics, where you work as a security analyst. Your job: find out which Tarnhelm-relevant passwords can be recovered from the dump's hashes so the owners can be forced to rotate them.

The dump in ~/cases/larkspur/hashes.lst holds five unsalted MD5 and SHA-1 hashes. Build rainbow tables, sort them, and run the cracker.

Tarnhelm password policy

  • At least 12 characters long.
  • Includes both upper- and lower-case letters.
  • Contains at least one of: ! " # $ % & _ ' * @
