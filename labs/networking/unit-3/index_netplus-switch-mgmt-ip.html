Bring a Branch Access Switch Online

N10-009 2.2 · 3.5 · 1.7  |  AP Networking 3.3.C.3 · 3.3.A.5

The Tidewater Makers Collective just finished wiring the upstairs loft workshop. The new access switch MK-SW-LOFT powers on with a blank management config — it can switch frames, but no one can reach it remotely to monitor or update it.

Your job: give the switch a reachable identity on the management network and a way out. The upstream router MK-RTR-CORE is already up and advertising itself over LLDP — use that to find your gateway, then make the change stick across a reboot.
